# Consultix service positioning — research and editorial rationale
Date: 29 October 2026
Status: internal review note, not a claim of delivered client work

## Strategic conclusion
Use the five practices as individual entry points with a coherent shared theme: financial planning and management reporting, RevOps/CRM, BI/analytics, strategic Revenue-to-Finance Alignment, and AI/workflow improvement. The advantage is not the number of practices but the ability to trace business decisions through commercial activity, operations, reporting, profit and cash.

### Why these practices
1. **FP&A and management reporting** address planning uncertainty, financial scenarios and management decisions. Gartner's FP&A guidance highlights integrated financial and operational planning and rolling forecasts; Deloitte's Finance Trends 2026 identifies scenario planning as a major leadership priority.
2. **RevOps and CRM** address consistent lifecycle definitions, systems and cross-functional commercial handoffs. Gartner's 2025 RevOps research focuses on aligned commercial execution. HubSpot's September 2026 RevOps tool guide distinguishes the core CRM from a broader technology/process stack.
3. **Business intelligence** is not merely dashboard creation. The purpose is to establish trusted definitions and investigate drivers of financial, commercial and operational performance. IBM's February 2026 FP&A analysis notes that integrated planning connects financial and operational drivers.
4. **Revenue-to-Finance Alignment** is the strategic differentiator. Sales pipeline, bookings, recognised revenue, receivables and collected cash are not interchangeable. Contract terms and accounting policies determine revenue recognition; projects should help establish reliable handoffs, assumptions and analysis, not impersonate a qualified auditor or accountant. See IFRS 15.
5. **AI enablement** should be a measured improvement service rather than a promise of automatic ROI. McKinsey's 2026 global AI survey points to workflow redesign and measurement as features of organisations achieving more value.

### Distinctions and example measures
| Practice | Primary management question | Representative KPIs / measures | Typical outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Financial Planning & Management Reporting | What can we afford or change? | Budget variance, forecast accuracy, gross margin, burn, cash outlook | Documented model, scenarios, commentary and refresh routine |
| Revenue Operations Management & CRM | Can commercial work be tracked and executed reliably? | Stage conversion, pipeline coverage, sales cycle, renewal follow-up, CRM completeness | CRM/RevOps review, lifecycle rules, ownership and workflows |
| Business Intelligence & Data Analytics | What is actually driving performance across functions? | Financial vs CRM reconciliations, cohorts, capacity, profitability by segment, data quality | KPI dictionary, source map, diagnostic analysis and reporting |
| Revenue-to-Finance Alignment | How does commercial growth translate into results and cash? | Bookings-to-recognition differences, collection timing, DSO where appropriate, customer/project margin | Revenue bridge, process map, agreed assumptions and planning alignment |
| AI Enablement & Automation | Is this change worth implementing and maintaining? | Cycle time, error/review rates, adoption, per-workflow costs | Readiness review, one measured pilot and handover |

These examples are **not universal benchmarks, promised outcomes, client results or regulated advice**. The exact measures depend on industry, business model, data quality and scope.

## Client buying structure
The paid Business Performance & Planning Review is the scope-setting entry offer when the problem is still uncertain. A direct project can be scoped when the requirements are already clear. The five project offers map one-to-one to the five practices. A startup planning variant and bounded monthly support are optional follow-ons, not additional named practices.

## Editorial and interaction decisions
Keep connected, natural English and formal German; avoid slogans, overly technical claims and unsupported credentials. A three-perspective connected performance lens illustrates financial, commercial and operational viewpoints using native HTML details. Keep existing scenario forecast, KPI explorer, audience guide, RevOps-to-finance flow and challenge selector intact. Fake testimonials remain hidden.
