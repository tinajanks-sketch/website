STRATEGIC FINANCE & FINANCIAL INTELLIGENCE

Financial planning that keeps pace with your business.

A budget is useful when it helps you make the next decision. As sales, costs and priorities change, your financial outlook needs to reflect what is actually happening.

We connect financial plans with commercial and operational drivers, investigate the reasons behind performance changes and help your team maintain a useful view of the months ahead.

01

Budgeting & rolling forecasts

When an annual budget no longer reflects the business, revisit its revenue, cost and resource assumptions. We build driver-based budgets, rolling forecasts and scenarios around the decisions your team needs to make.

What you receiveA planning model with documented assumptions, agreed scenarios and a practical monthly update process.

02

Variance analysis & reporting

Conflicting reports and unexplained movements make it difficult to act. We compare actual results with budget and forecast, examine timing and cost drivers, and agree on performance measures that support management decisions.

What you receiveBudget-versus-actual reporting, clear variance commentary and a management reporting pack built around your priorities.

03

Cash flow & profitability

Revenue growth can put pressure on cash, and strong sales can hide weak margins. We examine cash timing and profitability by an agreed lens, such as client, project or service, to help evaluate spending, pricing and capacity choices.

What you receiveA cash-flow outlook and profitability analysis, with the assumptions and limitations explained.

How this example is calculated

Forecast revenue starts from the latest actual month and compounds at the selected monthly growth rate. Variable costs are the selected share of forecast revenue.

Forecast fixed overheads start at EUR 42,000 per month, adjusted by the selected percentage. Operating result equals revenue minus variable costs and fixed overheads.

The original budget assumes monthly revenue growth of 2%, variable costs of 40% and fixed overheads of EUR 42,000. Actual figures are a separate, fixed sample dataset. All calculations round to whole euros each month. This simplified model excludes taxes, financing and the timing of cash collections or payments; operating result is not cash flow.

All figures are hypothetical and illustrate the method. They are not client results or a forecast for your business.

The aim is to make assumptions visible, explain the gaps between plan and performance, and give your team a clearer basis for decisions. The scope and fee are agreed before work begins.
